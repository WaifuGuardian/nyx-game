# NYX RPG — PILLARS
*Reread this whenever scope creeps. If a feature doesn't serve a pillar, it waits.*

1. **It is Nyx's story.** Main quests and side quests follow her; rarely switch POV.
2. **Growth is done by XP.** XP comes from defeating enemies, completing quests,
   or progressing the story. Leveling increases stats and unlocks features.
3. **Main resources:** Health, Mana, Stamina, Hunger.
4. **Consequence is legible.** Stealing gets you caught; being caught costs
   reputation/alignment; low alignment means restrictions but also opportunities.
5. **Weird/unique features:** random events during travel, ability to recruit
   most enemies, dressing Nyx up (outfits give stats), romance with certain NPCs,
   spellcrafting a la Morrowind, abilities tied to skills not class.
